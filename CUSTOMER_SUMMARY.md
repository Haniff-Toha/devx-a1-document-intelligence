# Kirana Logistics Group
## Annual-report assistant

### What it does

The investor relations team can ask questions about Kirana's annual report in plain English. The assistant provides a short answer and points to the relevant page, table, chart, or passage. It can follow a correction to the original statement, so an analyst can see which figure to use and why. When it cannot support an answer from the report, it says so.

### How it performed

On 18 test questions, the assistant gave correct answers with supporting citations for 13 of the 14 questions the report could answer. It declined all four questions whose answers were absent, including a request for a discount from a schedule outside the report. It read the scanned contract tariff and found both the storage rate and the VAT note. It declined one safety question because it could not verify a year in its draft against the passage it cited.

### Where it needs care

The assistant works with one report at a time. Complex layouts and blurry scans can cause it to miss information or decline a question that someone could answer by reading the page. A page citation makes a claim easier to check. Staff should verify material figures against the cited page before using an answer externally. Automated answers require an internet connection and send the relevant report content to a hosted service.

### Recommended next step

Pilot it with the investor relations team using a second report and a sample of real analyst questions. Track whether reviewers can verify an answer quickly, how often useful answers are withheld, and whether chart or tariff figures need correction. Use those results to decide which document layouts to support before broadening access.
